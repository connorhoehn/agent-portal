## 2. aws-agentcore shape

- One shared shell serves all four tenants (`portal/src/components/shell/Layout.tsx`). It holds `ActivityBell` (`:452`), `ShellCallHost` and `ShellCallDock` (`:534`), `EmbeddedSocialProvider` and `EmbeddedSocialEntry` (`:404`, `:535`), and gateway and notification providers (`App.tsx:196-197`).
- The embedded social entry only runs for sessions holding a social grant (`EmbeddedSocial.tsx:53` `eligible`); other tenants fall back to the tenant-neutral `TenantDirectEntry` (`:152`).
- The gateway mounts `chat`, `presence`, `calls`, `notifications`, `reactions`, `cursor` (`lib/gateway-cursor.ts`: the router's channel authority decides, identity is stamped from the socket's auth context) (`realtime-gateway.ts:815`, store in `gateway-attachments.ts`) and `fileUploads` (`realtime-gateway.ts:819`; bytes over `PUT/GET /api/uploads/:id`, `gateway-attachments.ts` `createUploadsRouter`, mounted `index.ts:251`). `activity` and `pipeline` are now mounted (`realtime-gateway.ts`, wrappers in `lib/gateway-run-feed.ts`): activity is server-produced only (client `publish` refused, no global broadcast auto-subscribe, `getHistory` gated by the subscribe check); the library's `pipeline:run:<id>` wire channel is subscribed as the `pipelines:run:<id>` family and the `pipeline:all`/`pipeline:approvals` firehoses are not offered. Producers: run status moves (`providers/pipeline-definitions.ts` `updateRecord`) and work-item assign/status (`routes/work-items.ts`, on `orgiq:initiative:<id>`). `rooms` exists in the installed dist (`dist/server/index.d.ts:19`) but is not mounted. Canvas CRDT runs on the separate `/realtime` path.
- Channel authority is now per family (`lib/tenant-channels.ts:94` `tenantChannelFamily`, `:100` `channelAuthorityFor`): a default-deny allowlist for `orgiq:invoke|session|initiative|work`, `assessment:doc|knowledge`, `pipelines:run|def|deck` (decks presence-only, with `assessment:deck`) and `<tenant>:lobby`. `channelGuard`/`lobbyGuard` check the shape (`realtime-gateway.ts:224,257`); `live-principal.ts:184-186,239` re-asks the family's Cedar action against the reloaded session on every subscribe/publish/lobby join. Social keeps its own membership checks there. Unit-tested; not exercised in a browser.
- Tenant-neutral presence and DMs: `GET /api/people/shared` (`routes/people.ts:29`) hands back `<tenant>:lobby` and the `<tenant>:dm:` prefix (`:47`); the portal mounts `TenantDirectEntry` (`TenantDirectMessages.tsx:38`) from `EmbeddedSocial.tsx:152` when the session holds no social grant, using `usePresence` (`:21`) and `useChat` + `WrappedChatPanel` (`:30`). Its past-DM list is now a `ConversationRail` fed by `GET /api/people/conversations`; DMs only; not browser-verified.
- Shared pins beyond Social (2026-10-06): `/api/chat/pins` and `/api/chat/message` accept `orgiq:initiative|work`, `orgiq:huddle:<callId>`, `pipelines:run|def` and `assessment:doc` (`routes/chat-pins.ts` `tenantPinAccess`). Each request asks `authorizeConversationChat` fresh (family read authority to list/Jump, its publish authority to pin/unpin, chat membership rows, a directory reload), re-asks after the message/author lookup, and derives excerpt/author/time from the stored original with the history floor and deletions applied. Bearer realtime token or cookie session; other families 400. Adversarial jest: `routes/__tests__/tenant-chat-pins.test.ts`.
- Pipeline cancel no longer notifies the canceller on the job-event path (`providers/pipeline-definitions.ts:359,373`).
- Portal `lib/capabilities.tsx` mirrors Cedar actions. It does not use the library's `useCapabilities` or `useFeatureFlag`.
